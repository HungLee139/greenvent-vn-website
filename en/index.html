About Green Vent

A small detail that decides whether a room looks finished

Green Vent International LLC is based in Austin, Texas. We make aluminum vent covers, floor registers, and return grilles for residential walls, ceilings, and floors, plus a line of linear slot diffusers.

Most vent covers on the market are plastic, which turns brittle and cracks. Aluminum does not crack, does not yellow, and holds its shape.
