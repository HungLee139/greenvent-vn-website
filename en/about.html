About

Green Vent International LLC

A U.S. company in Austin, Texas, focused on aluminum HVAC vent covers for modern homes.

What we do

Green Vent sells aluminum vent covers, floor registers, and return grilles for residential wall, ceiling, and floor openings, plus linear slot diffusers. Our customers are homeowners who care about quality and design and want to replace an old vent themselves.

Products are sold on greenvent.us and through official stores on Amazon, Walmart, and Etsy.

Invisible Comfort, Visible Quality.

How we work

Three things we hold to

01

Why aluminum

Most vent covers on the market are plastic, which turns brittle and cracks. Steel can rust where it is damp. Aluminum does not crack, does not yellow, and holds its shape for years.

02

Getting the size right

Most returns come from ordering the wrong size. So every product lists duct opening and face size separately, specs are checked against one reference sheet per line, and photos show the back and the mounting hardware.

03

How we operate

Green Vent is a small team with no call center. Orders on greenvent.us are fulfilled and supported by us directly. Marketplace orders follow each marketplace's own shipping and return process.
